The shadow war

Berlin is split by a war nobody talks about. The state - police, state security and army - hunts the resistance; the resistance prints leaflets, sends broadcasts and sabotages the machine. Every action counts for one side: /city shows the unrest and the score of the week.

Joining

  • Only civilians can join. Find Uncle Otto, an old man by the forest bridge, and press E.
  • Membership is secret - nobody can see it. You get 10 points as a welcome.
  • Otto's menu: jobs, the shop, a spare transmitter and your status.

Points and ranks

Resistance points are your currency at Otto. Your rank follows the points you earned in total:

The radio broadcast, step by step

  1. Buy a field transmitter from Otto (10 points).
  2. Install it in a vehicle.
  3. Drive across the forest bridge into the countryside - away from the city and the military posts.
  4. Type /transmit in or next to the vehicle.
  5. Keep the vehicle alive for 5 minutes. The police get an alarm and see where you are.

After a broadcast the airwaves are watched for 10 minutes before the next one can start.

Heat, arrests and betrayal

  • Heat: every risky action makes you more suspicious (a broadcast or sabotage adds about 20). The bar sits in the bottom left corner. At 80 an automatic warrant goes out.
  • Lying low: heat cools down over time, three times faster near Otto. An arrest clears it; forged papers set it to 20.
  • Searches: resistance material found on you is confiscated and adds heat.
  • Interrogation: the police can question you when you are restrained or jailed. Stay silent and the cell pays you back after your release - or talk, and the police raid the printing press, a dead drop or Otto's meeting place. A traitor is out of the resistance for a day.
  • Recruiting is a risk: whoever refuses might talk.

The cell

  • /signal pins a red carnation to your coat for a minute - only comrades see it.
  • /recruit (Agent): recruit the civilian you look at, face to face.
  • Uprising: at 85 % unrest Berlin erupts for 15 minutes - double points for the resistance, 150 RM for every resistance fighter the police or army jail. Afterwards the score shows who held the streets.

For the police and the army

  • A broadcast sets off an alarm with the vehicle's position - destroy it within 5 minutes.
  • Repair cut junction boxes (E, +150 RM) and sabotaged engines (/repairengine, +50 RM).
  • Hand in confiscated envelopes and files for a bonus.
  • Interrogate prisoners (/interrogate): a confession gives you a raid order with a marker.
  • Every arrest of a resistance member lowers the unrest and counts for the state.
