Keys and menus

Type commands in the chat. <name> = part of a character name or a SteamID, [ ] = optional.

F1

Main menu: character, inventory and the handbook with every rule and system.

F3

Recognise menu: let the people around you see your name.

E

Use NPCs, doors, phones, lockers and boxes. On a person: tie them up (needs restraints).

@ text / /report text

Opens a ticket. A staff member claims it and comes to help you.

Everyday commands

/guide

Introduction for new players.

/discord /forums /content /workshop /store

Links: Discord, forum, content collection, store.

/perks /ammotype <match|magnum>

Your store perks; switch the ammunition type.

/level /top /topmoney /casinotop

Service level and XP, leaderboards.

/chardesc <text>

Your character description.

/givemoney [name] <amount> /dropmoney <amount>

Give money to the person you look at, or drop it.

/showid /papers /showpapers [id|f|w|s|p]

Show your ID card and papers (p = party card, party tier IV and higher).

/phone /phonebook /mynumber /setvoicemail [text]

Telephone (E on a phone). 8000 is the anonymous tip line.

/roll [max]

Roll a die.

/anim 0-10 /anims /salute /sit <nr> /fallover [seconds]

Gestures and animations; /act... commands for sitting, standing and more.

/thirdperson /music

Third-person view, music.

/doorbuy /doorsell /doorkick

Buy and sell doors, kick one in.

/city

Unrest in Berlin and the weekly score of state against resistance.

/wanted /jailtime

Wanted list; time left in jail.

/mining

How the mine works.

Vehicles

Car dealer at the station square. Tabs: Private cars, Luxury, Premium, Commercial and Government. You need a driving licence from the Licence Office at the police station.

Luxury

Mercedes 770 and 290, Audi 920 and the fastest sports cars - for money and service level.

Premium

The 1950s collection for supporters (VIP or the premium vehicles package).

Government

Free for police, army and officials: Kubelwagen, Horch Kfz. 15, BMW R75 motorcycles, troop bicycle and Sd.Kfz. halftracks (the larger ones from NCO rank). State limousine and armoured van for officials.

Keys

Left click locks, right click unlocks the car you look at.

/carkeys <name> /carinfo

Give or take back a spare key; see who owns a car.

The resistance

Civilians only, and membership is secret. Join at Uncle Otto by the forest bridge (E): jobs, a shop (weapons, ammunition, transmitters, forged papers), spare transmitters and your status.

/transmit

In or at a vehicle with a field transmitter, across the forest bridge in the countryside and away from military posts. Keep the vehicle alive for 5 minutes. The next broadcast can start 10 minutes later.

Otto's jobs

Cut a phone line (the tip line goes dead), courier run to a dead drop, steal files from an office (Agent), sabotage a state vehicle (Agent).

/sabotage

Agent: sugar in the tank of a parked police or army vehicle - the engine will not start for 5 minutes.

/recruit

Agent: recruit the civilian you look at.

/signal

A red carnation for 60 seconds that only members can see.

Heat

The bar at the bottom left. It drops three times faster near Otto; at 80 an automatic warrant goes out.

Uprising

At 85 % unrest Berlin erupts for 15 minutes: double resistance points, rewards for every arrest.

Police and state security

/checkid

Ask for papers (on a tied-up person: read all of them).

/search

Search a tied-up person and seize contraband: weapons, drugs, resistance material.

/arrest <minutes> [reason] /unarrest <name>

Jail a tied-up person, or release them. Also /jail and /unjail.

/interrogate

Question a prisoner. If a member talks, you get a raid order with a marker.

/warrant <name> <reason> /unwarrant <name> /warrants

Warrants.

/tips

Anonymous tips from the 8000 line.

/repairengine

Repair a sabotaged vehicle (+50 RM).

/givelicense <name> /revokelicense <name>

Driving licences (Licence Office).

Raids

With a raid order, press E on the printing press, the dead drop or the contact.

Phone lines

E on a cut junction box repairs it (+150 RM).

Leadership and offices

/broadcast <text>

Radio announcement for the government offices; Local Group Leaders (party tier V) once an hour.

/sponsor

Local Group Leader: vouch for the civilian you look at - Tier I costs them 3,000 instead of 5,000 RM for 30 minutes.

/factionbroadcast <factions> <text>

Announcement to chosen factions (flag B).

/announce <text>

Announcement.

/pa [name]

Personnel file.

/medal [name] [medal] [reason] /medals [name] /unmedal

Award, show or take back medals.

/airraid

Air raid alarm.

Supporters

VIP and store packages pay for the server - see the Store page.

/pac

Opens the PAC3 outfit editor (VIP, the PAC3 package and admins). Combat parts, models from the internet, outfits on props and movement changes are switched off.

Internet images

Other players' PAC3 images from the internet only show if you switch them on: pac_enable_urltex 1.

/perks

Shows what you have and how long it lasts.
