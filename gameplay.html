Berlin, 1942

Valhalla Networks is a serious roleplay server on Garry's Mod. Every player is a person of the city: with papers, a job, money, a service record and a reputation. The factions run Berlin, the civilians keep it alive - and some of them fight the state in secret.

Everything is connected. A tip on the telephone leads to a warrant, the warrant to an arrest, the arrest to an interrogation, and the interrogation to a raid. Nothing needs a staff member to start: the city runs on its own.

Your first hour

  1. Create your character - name, face and clothes. You start as a citizen of Berlin.
  2. Find work at the Labour Office by the station: delivery driver, postman or road repairs.
  3. Earn money - pay arrives every 10 minutes, plus a daily login bonus that grows with your streak.
  4. Get your papers in order - ID card, driving licence (Licence Office at the police station), later a car and a flat.
  5. Join a faction - apply on our Discord; staff assign you through the personnel office.

Press F1 at any time: character, inventory, the full handbook and the rules.

Money and progress

  • Pay every 10 minutes: 80 RM for civilians, 120 to 500 RM by rank in a faction - straight into your bank account (a pay account is opened for you with the first paycheck). Seeding bonus: +50 % while fewer than 10 players are online.
  • Daily login bonus: 250 RM on the first day, up to 1,150 RM on the seventh day of a streak.
  • Service XP for active play. /level shows your level, /top the roll of honour.
  • Levels unlock things: bigger cars at the dealer need a service level.
  • Spend it on cars, doors, weapons, the Spielbank - or keep it at the bank.

Factions and ranks

Four faction groups run the city: Government, Party, Armed Forces and Security. Each has its own Discord, its own leadership and its own buildings.

  • Ranks (Enlisted, NCO, Officers, General officers) decide your uniform, your equipment and which vehicles you may take.
  • Quartermaster of your unit: weapons, magazines, field radio and restraints. Lockers: uniform by rank, insignia and kit.
  • Personnel file and medals: leaders award medals with a reason; they show on your character and in /medals.
  • Faction doors open for members and lock behind them.

Law and order

Papers matter. Police and state security check them, and a missing or forged paper can end in a cell.

  • Papers: ID card, driving licence, military pass, service ID. Show them with /showid or /showpapers.
  • Checks and arrests: restraints, /checkid, /search, jail with a reason and a time.
  • Wanted board: officers issue warrants; /wanted shows who is sought.
  • Tip line 8000: anyone can call anonymously from any telephone. A tip that leads to an arrest is paid.
  • Heat: suspicious people land on the wanted list on their own (see the resistance guide).

Vehicles

The car dealer at the station square sells private cars, commercial vehicles, luxury cars and - for supporters - the premium collection. Only the owner and holders of a spare key can drive; a wrecked car spends 3 minutes in the workshop.

The Government tab is the motor pool of the state: police, army and officials take Kubelwagen, Horch Kfz. 15, BMW R75 motorcycles, troop bicycles and Sd.Kfz. halftracks out for free. Larger vehicles need NCO rank.

The living city

Berlin has a mood. The unrest meter (/city) rises with every resistance success and falls with every arrest:

Calm (0-29 %)The state has the streets.
Restless (30-59 %)Leaflets on the walls, nervous patrols.
Unrest (60-84 %)The resistance is bold, the police are stretched.
Uprising (85 %+)15 minutes of open revolt - double resistance points, 150 RM per resistance fighter jailed.
  • City events start without staff: supply shortages (double pay at the Labour Office), air raids, police bounties and dark nights.
  • Weekly war score: every arrest, leaflet and broadcast counts for the state or the resistance. On Monday the winning side gets paid.

Everyday life

  • Telephones: buy a number at the Telephone Office, place your phone, call anyone from the phonebook.
  • Spielbank Berlin: slot machines with a jackpot, a free daily spin of the wheel of fortune, roulette, VIP levels.
  • Crafts: notes, books, cooking, farming, fishing and the mine all work through items.
  • Doors: buy a door with /doorbuy and the rooms behind it are yours.

Help

Type @ followed by your question in the chat: it opens a ticket and a staff member comes to you. The rules are on the Rules page and in F1; every command is on the Commands page.
